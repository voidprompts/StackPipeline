Most Airtable to Slack integrations fail for the same reason: they alert on every record
update, the channel fills with noise, and within two weeks everyone has muted it. The fix
is choosing the right trigger type from the start.

<Callout variant="shortcut" title="Use the native automation">
You do not need Zapier for this. Airtable's built-in Slack action is free on every plan,
including the free tier, and it is more reliable than routing through a third-party
automation platform.
</Callout>

## Choose your trigger carefully

This is the entire game. **When record updated** fires on every field change — including
the ones you make while editing. **When record matches conditions** fires only on the
transition into a matching state, which is almost always what you actually want.

## Writing alerts people actually read

A good alert answers three questions without requiring a click: what changed, what is it
worth, and where do I go to act on it. Include the record link in every message.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Route by value, not by table">
Send high-value events to a busy channel and everything else to a quieter one. A single
firehose channel gets muted; tiered routing survives.
</Callout>

## When to upgrade to Zapier or Make

Move off native automations when you need to fan out to multiple destinations, transform
data before sending, or chain conditional logic more than two levels deep. Below that
threshold, native is faster and free.
